VLON Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

VLON ("we", "us") is a laundry pickup and delivery app serving Pune, India. This policy explains what data we collect and why.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, phone number.
  • Address data: pickup and delivery addresses you enter.
  • Order data: items, amount, pickup date/slot, order status.
  • Payment data: payment method (COD or online). Online payments are processed by Razorpay; we do not store your card/UPI details.

2. Why we collect it

  • To create your account and log you in.
  • To pick up, clean and deliver your laundry.
  • To contact you about your order.
  • To process payments and prevent fraud.

3. Who we share it with

  • Our vendors and delivery staff (only what is needed to fulfil your order).
  • Google Firebase (authentication and database hosting).
  • Razorpay (payment processing).

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Data storage and security

Data is stored on Google Firebase servers (Mumbai region). Access is restricted by security rules and roles.

5. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your account and data by emailing us at the address below.

6. Children

VLON is not intended for children under 18.
